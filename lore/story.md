# OLYMPUS FALL
## A Game Universe Concept

---

## THE WORLD

### The Great Divide

In the near future, humanity achieved the pinnacle of bioengineering: **Synthetics** — humanoid beings crafted from artificial white flesh and synthetic blood. They are stronger, faster, and more resilient than humans, yet designed to serve.

But this miracle didn't unite humanity. It divided it further.

The world split into two realms:

**ELYSIUM (The Upper World)** — A floating archipelago of islands suspended above the clouds, home to the ultra-wealthy elite. Here, gleaming white marble towers rise against pristine skies, decorated with gold filigree and crimson banners. The citizens of Elysium worship beauty, perfection, and spectacle. Ancient Greek aesthetics merged with cutting-edge technology: columns that project holograms, amphitheaters with force-field arenas, temples housing quantum computers.

**THE UNDERCITY (The Lower World)** — The forgotten surface below, shrouded in perpetual shadow from the floating islands above. Mountains of discarded technology, broken Synthetics, and refuse rain down from Elysium. The people here are scavengers, surviving on scraps, trading salvage, and dreaming of a world they'll never see.

---

### The Synthetics

Synthetics are artificial beings grown in bio-vats, their bodies composed of a distinctive **white flesh** that distinguishes them from natural humans. Their blood runs pale blue. They feel no pain by default (though some are modified to feel it for "authenticity" in the arena). They can be programmed, modified, enhanced.

In Elysium, they serve as servants, laborers, companions — and **gladiators**.

---

### The Games

The greatest obsession of Elysium's elite: **The Arena**.

Gladiatorial combat between Synthetics is the supreme entertainment. Wealthy patrons — called **Domini** — own stables of fighters and compete for glory, influence, and astronomical sums. The trainers who shape these fighters are known as **Lanistae** (singular: Lanista).

The tournaments follow a strict hierarchy:
- **Street Pits** — Illegal fights in the Undercity, brutal and unregulated
- **The Bronze Circuit** — Entry-level sanctioned fights in Elysium's outer districts
- **The Silver Circuit** — Mid-tier tournaments, broadcast across Elysium
- **The Gold Circuit** — Elite competitions among established houses
- **The Olympiad** — The ultimate tournament, held once per year in the Grand Colosseum of Elysium Prime

## THE STORY

### Premise

You are **nobody**.

A denizen of the Undercity with no name worth remembering, no credits to your account, and no future worth imagining. You survive day-to-day, scavenging through the refuse that falls from the sky.

One night, something different falls.

A Synthetic — intact, functional, merely damaged. By some miracle, the Collectors haven't found it yet. These vultures usually strip any valuable salvage within hours. But this one landed in your territory. Your find.

A quick examination reveals superficial damage: scratches, a few dents, one arm slightly misaligned. But the core systems are operational. The eyes flicker to life — pale, questioning, waiting.

You have a choice. Sell it for parts and eat for a month. Or...

The Arena. 

The Street Pits aren't far. They say anyone can enter a Synthetic in the fights. Win enough, and you can buy your way into the Bronze Circuit. Win there, and doors begin to open. The kind of doors that lead **up**.

You decide to gamble everything on a broken machine and a desperate dream.

---

### Your Journey

From the mud of the Undercity to the marble halls of Elysium, you will:

- **Repair and enhance** your Synthetic using salvaged parts and black-market upgrades
- **Train** fighting styles, from brutal strength to elegant technique
- **Navigate** the criminal underworld of the Undercity and the political intrigue of Elysium
- **Build** your reputation from anonymous scavenger to legendary Lanista
- **Uncover** the mystery of why your Synthetic was discarded — and what secrets it carries
